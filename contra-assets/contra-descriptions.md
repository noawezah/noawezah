# Contra project descriptions

## PEBBLE — Café Website Design & Development

A warm, welcoming website for PEBBLE, a specialty café in Bucharest. Cream tones, expressive serif typography, layered photography, and playful snail details bring the café’s relaxed atmosphere online. The experience balances character with practical information, helping visitors discover the coffee, explore the space, and find opening hours and directions. Responsive layouts and English/Romanian language options make the site easy to use across devices.

Live website: https://pebble2.vercel.app/
Suggested tags: Web Design, Web Development, UI/UX Design

## URBANIST — Coffee, Culture & After Hours

An expressive website for Urbanist Sound House, bringing coffee, food, music, and underground culture into one shared digital space. Oversized typography, violet accents, textured backgrounds, and layered imagery reflect the venue’s energy. An explorable menu, sound and culture sections, social content, and clear location details help visitors discover the place and plan their next visit.

Live website: https://urbanist2.vercel.app/
Suggested tags: Web Design, Web Development, UI/UX Design

## noawezah — Personal Portfolio Design & Development

A personal portfolio built around the idea that a website should feel like the business behind it. Bold typography, a dark palette, and lime accents establish a distinct identity, while scroll-led transitions and desktop and mobile project previews bring the work into focus. The site connects selected projects, the design process, and a direct enquiry flow, giving potential clients a clear path from discovering the work to starting a conversation.

Live website: https://noawezah.ro/
Suggested tags: Web Design, Web Development, Portfolio Design
